# SaaS Business Performance Intelligence - Project Brief

### Business Context
---
A B2B software-as-a-service (SaaS) company generates recurring revenue from customers on different subscription plans. Management needs better visibility into subscription performance, customer retention, and product usage.

### Problem Statement
---
Management requires a consolidated view of business performance to understand how recurring revenue and the active customer base change over time, which customer segments contribute to performance, and where retention or product engagement may warrant further investigation.

### Project Objective
---
Develop a reproducible data analysis and business intelligence solution that converts customer, subscription, and product-usage data into reliable performance metrics and actionable management insights.

### Key Business Questions
---
* How have monthly recurring revenue (MRR) and active customers changed over the reporting period?

* Which customer segments and acquisition channels contribute most to recurring revenue?

* What is the monthly customer churn rate, and how does it vary across segments?

* How does product engagement differ across customer segments and subscription plans?

* Are usage patterns or support activity associated with customer churn?

* Which findings should management investigate first?

### Deliverables
---
* Data quality audit and documented metric definitions.

* SQL queries for core business questions.

* Python-based data validation and exploratory analysis.

* Excel reporting components.

* Tableau executive dashboard.

* Business recommendations and an executive summary.

### Scope & Limitations
---
The project uses synthetic data for portfolio development. Findings describe the simulated dataset and are not presented as actual company performance. Associations between customer behavior and churn do not establish causation.

### Definition of Success
---
Management should be able to understand the company's performance trends, investigate differences between customer segments, and identify evidence-based areas for further action.
